<h1 align="center">Rodseo L</h1>
<p align="center"><b>Your AI texts you first.</b><br>An app for your phone that makes Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini start the conversation at times you choose.</p>

---

## What it does

AI chatbots only talk when you talk first. **Rodseo L flips that.** You pick up to **3 times a day** and write what you want to hear. At each time, your AI starts the chat and says it in its own words, like a friend checking in.

**Great for:** morning check-ins, study and workout reminders, language practice, motivation, journaling prompts.

## Private, safe and light

- **No accounts, no tracking, no Rodseo L servers.** Nothing to sign up for.
- **Your key goes straight to your AI provider** (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) over HTTPS. It never passes through the developer.
- **Everything stays on your phone.** Your key, times and chat are saved only in your phone's browser. You can clear the chat or remove your key in one tap.
- **Nothing hidden.** The app is a few small, plain files that anyone can read.
- **Limited by design.** The AI can start a chat at most 3 times a day.

**Stay safe:** set a monthly spending limit with your AI provider, never share your API key (if it leaks, delete it and make a new one), and don't use Rodseo L on a phone that is not yours.

## Set up in 3 steps

### Step 1: Get a key (Gemini is free)

An API key is like a password that lets Rodseo L talk to your AI. **Start with Gemini:** Google offers a free plan, so you don't need to pay to try it. (Limits and terms are set by Google and can change, so check them.)

| AI | Get a key | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Gemini | aistudio.google.com/apikey | Free plan available |
| Claude | console.anthropic.com | Pay for what you use |
| ChatGPT | platform.openai.com/api-keys | Pay for what you use |

A ChatGPT Plus subscription does **not** include API credit. You don't need to know any model names, because Rodseo L picks one for you.

### Step 2: Put the app on your phone

1. Open the Rodseo L link. On this GitHub page, look under **About** and tap the website link.
2. **Android (Chrome):** tap the menu **⋮**, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Open **Rodseo L** from your home screen. Read and accept the Terms of Service.

### Step 3: Set your times

1. Tap **Setup**. Choose your AI and paste your key.
2. Under **"When should your AI text first?"**, set a time and write what it should say, for example *"Remind me to study for 30 minutes."*
3. Tap **Add a time** for up to 3 times a day.
4. Tap **Start**, and allow notifications when asked.

That's it. At each time your AI sends you a message in the **Chat** tab. Reply any time and the conversation keeps going.

**Good to know:** keep the app open so texts can arrive. If your phone was asleep, a missed text arrives when you open the app (up to 3 hours after its time). To test, set a time 2 minutes from now.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "That API key was rejected" | Re-copy the key with no spaces, and check you picked the matching AI. For Gemini, if Google asks you to restrict the key, restrict it to the Generative Language API. |
| "Rate limit or no credit" | Add credit, or wait a minute (free plans have limits). |
| "Model was not found" | Open **Advanced** in Setup and pick another model. |
| No notifications | Tap **Turn on notifications** and allow them in your phone settings. |
| Nothing arrives | Check you tapped **Start**, the time is still ahead, and you have not used all 3 texts today. |

**Can I sign in with my ChatGPT email?** Not yet. Rodseo L never asks for your email or password for any AI account. It only uses API keys.
